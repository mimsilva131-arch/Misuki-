Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Misuki handles information when you use the Misuki website and Discord bot.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on the features you use, Misuki may process information required for the bot and website to operate.

This may include Discord-related identifiers, server identifiers and configuration data.

3. Discord Information

When you authenticate through Discord, information provided by Discord may be used to identify your account and determine which servers you can manage.

Misuki only uses the permissions and information necessary for the available functionality.

4. Server Data

Server configuration data may be stored so that Misuki can remember settings between sessions.

Examples include configuration options, role identifiers, channel identifiers and other settings required by the bot.

5. How Information Is Used

Information is used to provide, maintain and improve Misuki's features.

Misuki does not require personal information unrelated to the operation of its services.

6. Data Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect stored information against unauthorized access or misuse.

7. Third-Party Services

Misuki may rely on third-party services, including Discord, for authentication and platform functionality.

Those services operate under their own privacy policies and terms.

8. Data Retention

Information may be retained for as long as it is necessary to provide the relevant functionality or maintain the service.

9. Your Rights

If you have questions about information associated with your use of Misuki, you can contact the support team.

10. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the Misuki service or its data practices change.

11. Contact

For questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please visit the Misuki support page.
