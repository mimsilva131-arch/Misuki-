Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Misuki collects, uses, stores and protects information when you use our services.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on the features you use, Misuki may process information provided by Discord or generated through your interaction with the service.

This may include information such as your Discord user ID, username, server information and configuration data required for Misuki to operate.

2. Discord Authentication

Misuki may use Discord OAuth2 to authenticate users.

When you sign in with Discord, Discord may provide information permitted by the OAuth2 scopes requested by Misuki.

Misuki does not receive or store your Discord password.

3. How We Use Information

Information may be used to:

  • Provide and operate Misuki features.
  • Authenticate users.
  • Maintain server configurations.
  • Improve reliability and security.
  • Respond to support requests.

4. Data Storage

Some information may be stored in databases required for the operation of Misuki.

We aim to retain only information that is necessary for providing the service.

5. Data Sharing

Misuki does not sell personal information.

Information may be processed by infrastructure or service providers when necessary to operate Misuki.

6. Security

Reasonable technical measures are used to protect stored information against unauthorized access, alteration or disclosure.

However, no online service can guarantee absolute security.

7. Data Retention

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, unless a longer retention period is required by law.

8. Your Rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights regarding your personal information, including rights to access, correct or request deletion of your data.

For data-related requests, please contact Misuki support.

9. Children's Privacy

Misuki is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable laws.

10. Third-Party Services

Misuki may interact with third-party services, including Discord.

Those services have their own privacy policies and terms that may apply to your use of them.

11. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when necessary. Changes will be reflected on this page.

12. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or your data, please contact Misuki support.
