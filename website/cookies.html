Cookie Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Misuki may use cookies and similar technologies to provide and maintain the website and its features.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small pieces of data stored by your browser when you visit a website. They can be used to remember information between requests and maintain sessions.

2. How Misuki Uses Cookies

Misuki may use cookies for purposes such as:

  • Maintaining authentication sessions.
  • Remembering necessary website preferences.
  • Protecting authenticated sessions.
  • Supporting essential website functionality.

3. Essential Cookies

Some cookies may be necessary for the website to function correctly. These cookies may be used for authentication, security and session management.

4. Third-Party Services

Misuki may use third-party services that can set or access cookies or similar technologies when their functionality is used.

These services may have their own privacy and cookie policies.

5. Managing Cookies

Most modern browsers allow you to control or delete cookies through their settings.

Disabling certain cookies may affect authentication or other essential features of the Misuki website.

6. Changes to This Policy

This Cookie Policy may be updated when necessary. Any changes will be reflected on this page.

7. Contact

If you have questions about cookies used by Misuki, please contact Misuki support.
