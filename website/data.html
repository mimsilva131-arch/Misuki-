Your Data

Manage your Misuki data

Misuki stores certain information required to provide its features. This page explains the types of information that may be associated with your account or Discord server.

What Data May Be Stored?

  • Discord user ID
  • Discord server IDs
  • Server configuration
  • Misuki feature settings
  • Information required for licenses and subscriptions
  • Support or review information that you voluntarily provide

Why Is It Stored?

This information allows Misuki to provide its functionality, remember server settings, authenticate users and maintain the service.

Requesting Your Data

You may contact Misuki support if you would like to request information about the data associated with your account.

Contact Support

Requesting Deletion

If you would like your data to be deleted, contact Misuki support and provide enough information for us to identify the relevant account or server.

Some information may need to be retained where required by law or where necessary for security, fraud prevention or legitimate technical purposes.

Discord Data

Misuki uses Discord authentication where applicable. Misuki does not store your Discord password.

Information received through Discord is handled according to the Misuki Privacy Policy and the permissions requested during authentication.
